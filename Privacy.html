1. Scope & who we are

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") applies to personal data processed by BrainTree HR Consulting Private Limited and its affiliates ("BrainTree HR", "we", "us", "our") through our website, recruitment and staffing services, applications, and related communications (collectively, the "Services").

Depending on the context and applicable law, BrainTree HR acts as a data fiduciary / controller / business when we determine the purposes and means of processing (for example, running our own recruitment operations), and as a data processor / service provider when we process personal data on behalf of a client under a written agreement.

Our registered office is at #19, 5th Floor, Shivashankar Plaza, Richmond Circle, Bengaluru – 560027, Karnataka, India. We also serve clients and candidates in the United States.

2. Data we collect

We collect the categories of personal data below, directly from you, from our clients, from your use of the Services, and from third parties such as job boards, references, and background-check providers where permitted by law.

From candidates

  • Identifiers & contact details — name, email, phone, postal address, and government identifiers only where required for onboarding or legal compliance.
  • Professional information — CV/résumé, employment history, education, skills, certifications, references, salary expectations, work authorization and eligibility.
  • Assessment data — interview notes, test or screening results, and recruiter evaluations.
  • Sensitive personal data — only where necessary and permitted (e.g. information relevant to workplace accommodations or diversity reporting), and, where required, with your consent.

From clients & business contacts

  • Business contact details, role and company, and information exchanged to fulfil an engagement.

From website visitors

  • Device and usage data — IP address, browser type, pages viewed, referring URLs, and similar analytics collected via cookies and comparable technologies (see Section 6).

3. How we use data

  • To provide recruitment, staffing, and placement services and match candidates to roles.
  • To communicate with you about applications, roles, and enquiries.
  • To perform contracts with clients and candidates and to administer accounts.
  • To conduct, where lawful and relevant, background, reference, and eligibility checks.
  • To operate, secure, analyze, and improve our Services and website.
  • To comply with legal, tax, and regulatory obligations and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
  • To send service updates and, where permitted, relevant career or hiring communications (you may opt out at any time).

We do not sell your personal data for money. Certain data-sharing for advertising analytics may be treated as a "sale" or "sharing" under some U.S. state laws — see Sections 12–13 for how to opt out.

4. Legal bases for processing

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following: your consent; performance of a contract with you; compliance with a legal obligation; and our legitimate interests (or the "legitimate uses" recognized under India's DPDP Act), such as operating and securing our Services, provided these are not overridden by your rights. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing.

5. How we share data

  • Clients & prospective employers — candidate profiles are shared with relevant hiring clients as part of the recruitment process.
  • Service providers / processors — vendors that host, support, analyze, or secure our Services, bound by contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Background & verification providers — where relevant to a role and permitted by law.
  • Legal & safety — to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, enforce agreements, or protect rights, property, and safety.
  • Corporate transactions — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset transfer, subject to this Policy.

6. Cookies & tracking technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to run the website, remember preferences, and understand usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where offered, our cookie banner. We honor recognized opt-out preference signals, including the Global Privacy Control (GPC), where required by law (see Sections 12–13).

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including to provide the Services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Candidate data may be retained to consider you for future roles; you may ask us to delete it as described below. Retention periods vary by data type and legal requirement.

8. Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; where a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify affected persons and authorities as required by applicable law (including the DPDP Act and applicable U.S. state breach-notification statutes).

9. International data transfers

Because we operate in India and the United States, your personal data may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards and contractual protections for such transfers.

10. Your rights in India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

If you are a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we process about you and the processing activities.
  • Correction, completion, updating, and erasure of your personal data.
  • Grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (Section 17).
  • Nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

We process personal data on the basis of your consent or recognized "legitimate uses" under the DPDP Act. You may contact our Grievance Officer and, if unsatisfied, lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India. We also continue to observe applicable obligations under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules regarding sensitive personal data.

11. United States — privacy notice

The United States does not have a single comprehensive federal privacy law; instead, sector-specific federal laws and a growing set of state laws apply. We comply with applicable U.S. federal requirements — including the CAN-SPAM Act for commercial email (every marketing message includes an unsubscribe mechanism), the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) for certain calls and texts, and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) — and with the state laws described below. Residents of certain states have additional rights, set out in Sections 12–14. To exercise any U.S. privacy right, contact us using Section 17; we will verify your request and respond within the timeframe required by the applicable state law (generally 45 days, extendable once).

Your California rights

  • Right to know / access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, disclose, and share.
  • Right to delete personal information we collected from you, subject to exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information, including for cross-context behavioral advertising. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as a valid opt-out.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information to what is necessary to provide the Services.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.
  • Authorized agent — you may use an authorized agent to submit requests; we may require verification.
  • "Shine the Light" — you may request information about disclosures of personal information to third parties for their direct-marketing purposes.

To exercise these rights, contact us via Section 17 or a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link where displayed. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16 years of age without opt-in consent.

13. Texas privacy rights (Texas Data Privacy and Security Act)

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA), effective 1 July 2024, gives you the right to:

  • Confirm and access whether we process your personal data and obtain a copy.
  • Correct inaccuracies in your personal data.
  • Delete personal data provided by or obtained about you.
  • Data portability — obtain a copy in a portable, readily usable format.
  • Opt out of (i) targeted advertising, (ii) the sale of personal data, and (iii) certain profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects. We recognize universal opt-out mechanisms such as GPC.

We do not sell sensitive or biometric personal data, and we obtain consent before processing sensitive data where the TDPSA requires it. You may appeal a refusal of your request by replying to our decision; if the appeal is denied, you may contact the Office of the Texas Attorney General. We will respond to requests within 45 days (extendable by an additional 45 days where reasonably necessary).

14. Other U.S. state rights

Residents of other states with comprehensive privacy laws — including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Oregon, Montana, and others as they take effect — have similar rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy of, and opt out of targeted advertising, sale, and certain profiling. We extend these rights to residents of those states as required by their laws. Contact us via Section 17 to make a request.

15. Children's privacy

Our Services are intended for individuals seeking employment and for business users, and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the minimum age required by applicable law) without verifiable parental consent, consistent with COPPA and the DPDP Act's protections for children. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date reflects the latest revision. Material changes will be highlighted on this page and, where required, communicated to you. Your continued use of the Services after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy where permitted by law.
