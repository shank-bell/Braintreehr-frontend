Our Mission

People aren't a funnel. They're the whole point.

We started BrainTree because we'd been on both sides of broken recruiting — and we knew it could be better. Better feedback, fairer salaries, clearer process, real follow-through.

Today we work with 150+ companies and a 1 Million person community of professionals. The numbers matter, but what we measure ourselves on is simpler: does the placement still feel like the right call after a year?

What we believe

Six things we won't compromise on

Our values aren't a poster on the wall. They're how we decide which clients we take on and how we treat every candidate.

01

Transparency by default

Every interview process explained. No bait-and-switch on roles or comp.

02

Reply to everyone

Even a no is a real human reply within 5 business days. Ghosting is a culture failure, not a process one.

03

Bet on potential

Pattern matching on resumes is lazy. We look for trajectory, curiosity, and how someone learns.

04

Long-term, not commission

The incentives finally line up with you.

05

Diversity as inputs

Diverse pipelines, structured interviews, scorecards, calibration. Not a slogan — a method.

06

Pick up the phone

Your recruiter's mobile number is on day one. Use it. We'd rather talk than email.
